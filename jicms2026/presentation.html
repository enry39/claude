🔐 Digital Gatekeeping ★ NEW

Four archetypal gatekeepers in the digital era: journalists, algorithms, aggregators, and trade press. Multiple co-existing logics.

Netflix markets local authenticity (dialect, setting, social issues) as a global commercial asset. Local = premium product.

Platformisation: What It Means for News

①Platform logics penetrate news production: publication rhythms, headline choices, topic selection now shaped by algorithmic distribution and engagement metrics.

②Streaming platforms are a fourth logic within the Hybrid Media System — not present when Chadwick (2013) wrote his book.

③Empirical evidence: Hase et al. (2022, Digital Journalism) show news outlets adapt publication rhythms to platform affordances across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Twitter. [137 citations]

④Nielsen et al. (2023, EJC) show platformisation varies by national context — directly relevant to IT vs. EN comparison.

Key argument: News outlets do not cover Mare Fuori because it is editorially newsworthy. They cover it because Netflix makes it visible. Platform logic has colonised the editorial calendar.

Nielsen et al. (2023) — 46 Countries

"Platformisation of news varies by national context and depends on the history of media systems." → Explains part of the IT/EN asymmetry we find.

European Journal of Communication [26 cit.]

Methodology: Media Cloud

Discussion: Four Key Findings

①

The Asymmetry IS the Finding

7,840 vs. 176 is not a methodological gap. It maps the real differential penetration of Italian cultural production into non-Italian media ecosystems. Netflix does not automatically globalise news coverage.

②

Platform Events Structure Editorial Calendars

Spike patterns track Netflix windows exactly. The Hybrid Media System now integrates streaming platform calendars as a fourth actor. News outlets react to platform events, not cultural salience.

③

International Gatekeeping: Three Logics

Trade press = industrial legitimation. Tabloid = spectacularisation. Quality press = cultural canonisation. Wallace (2018) digital gatekeeping model confirmed empirically across 176 articles.

④

Conspicuous Localism in Action

EN framing: "authentically Neapolitan" + "globally consumable via Netflix". The tension between local authenticity and cosmopolitan appeal is the product itself. Havens (2018) · Khazoom (2025).

Methodological Transparency: Declared Limitations

HIGH — Source Bias: rai.it / Televideo

46.2% of the Italian corpus (3,623 articles) originates from rai.it, a large proportion of which are machine-generated Televideo episode listings. Inflates counts without editorial value. Filtering would reduce corpus to ~4,217 and alter findings. Declared limitation, not hidden flaw.

MED — Query Noise: "The Sea Beyond"

English-language query includes the international title. Introduces travel/tourism content (~10–15% of Research B results may be off-topic). Manual review of sample would allow quantification and correction.

MED — Corpus Size Asymmetry

7,840 vs. 176 articles limits statistical comparability. Ratio-based metrics preferred to raw counts. The asymmetry itself, however, is substantively meaningful as the primary finding.

LOW — Headline/Metadata Analysis Only

Media Cloud operates on headlines and metadata, not full article text. Captures attention framing but not editorial depth or argument structure. Full-text analysis = next step.

What Computational Analyses Does This Study Perform?

① Temporal Frequency Analysis

Article counts per year and month. Identifies attention spikes and correlates them with platform events (Netflix premiere dates). Confirms platform calendar hypothesis.

Tool: Media Cloud API · Time series

② Source Distribution Analysis

Identifies dominant publishers. Reveals structural biases (rai.it/Televideo) and gatekeeping architecture (trade/tabloid/quality tripartition in EN corpus).

Tool: Media Cloud source metrics

③ Word Frequency / Framing Proxy

Top terms in headlines as computational proxy for editorial framing. Distinguishes broadcast logic (IT: "ascolti", "puntata") from platform logic (EN: "netflix", "exclusive").

Walter et al. (2019) ANTMN method

④ Cross-National Comparative Analysis

Systematic comparison of two corpora (IT vs. EN). Ratio-based metrics instead of raw counts. The asymmetry (97.8%/2.2%) as substantive finding rather than methodological artefact.

Nielsen et al. (2023) EJC as comparative framework

⑤ Event-Driven Correlation

Correlating Netflix release dates with publication spikes in both corpora. Identifies the platform calendar mechanism and the delayed EN lag (4–8 weeks). Confirms platformisation thesis empirically.

Conclusions: Three Propositions

①Mare Fuori's trajectory in the digital news ecosystem mirrors its platformisation trajectory: a national cultural product remade by platform logic into an international media object. The news does not lead; it follows the platform.

②Italian media attention is structurally determined by Netflix release windows. The Hybrid Media System (Chadwick 2013) now integrates streaming platform calendars into editorial routines as a fourth actor type. Platform logic has colonised the editorial calendar.

③English-language coverage is dominated by trade press industrial logic and tabloid spectacularisation — two distinct gatekeeping functions that together constitute the international media life of an Italian TV series. Quality press appearances are rare but symbolically decisive (cultural canonisation).

Next steps: Manual content analysis (excl. Televideo) · Broader language comparison (FR/DE/ES) · Integration with TikTok corpus (NECS 2026) · Full framing analysis via ANTMN (Walter et al. 2019) · Audience studies
